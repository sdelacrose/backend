REVISION AND IDENTITY RULES
Author launch signs atomically through the API transaction. A controller launch
waits for the assigned author's own signature. Author and verifier must differ;
verifier/approver may coincide, and author/approver is allowed in two steps.
An active workflow blocks version creation, withdrawal and deletion. Rejection needs
a reason, positive decisions do not. A stopped/rejected workflow remains immutable;
correction proceeds through a new version, never rewriting its historical signatures.

ARCHIVE
SMTP is a copy prepared by LINKS, not a Microsoft original or delivery receipt.
Graph originals are matched by signed per-attempt marker and verified recipients,
subject and attachment hashes. Archive failures never cause a resend. Earlier sends
are not automatically backfilled. No transactional outbox/WORM claim is made.
